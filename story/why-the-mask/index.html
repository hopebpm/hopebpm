WHY THE
MASK?

For HOPE BPM, the mask represents privacy, humility, and a new beginning.

Its white color represents purity and cleansing that come from the decision to walk forward on the foundation of God’s Word.

The smile comes from the joy God gives me, the joy of knowing that no matter what we face, we are never alone.

That realization takes away a tremendous weight. And that is why the mask always smiles.

The mask is a reminder that THE MESSAGE MATTERS MORE THAN THE ARTIST.

HOPE BPM is not about a person. It is about spreading the Word of God through modern music in a different way and reaching a new audience.

The black clothing also has a meaning. It represents our imperfections, our flaws, and the mistakes of our past.

We are not perfect, even as Christians. But God can take those burdens away, make us new, and put a smile back on our faces.

It’s not to change the message. Just the vehicle.

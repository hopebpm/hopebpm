HOPE BPM

MORE THAN
A DJ.

HOPE BPM is a Christian electronic artist and DJ shaped by more than a decade behind the decks and a lifelong connection to music.

Music has never been just background noise. It has been part of the journey — something to create, experience and share.

THEN SOMETHING CHANGED

WHEN THE
SOUND
CHANGED.

Years of DJing and exposure to high volumes eventually led to significant hearing loss — changing the way music had to be experienced and created.

For someone who had spent so much of life around music, that could have been the end of the story.

But the music never stopped.

FAITH

“I’ve been surrounded by music since I was a kid.”

“But it wasn’t until an incident affected one of my ears that Jesus gave me the strength to keep going.”

That experience changed more than the way music was heard. It changed the reason for making it.
